Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

About School Digest

School Digest is a personal application that helps users organize and summarize school-related information from their Google account.

Information We Access

School Digest may access Google user data only after the user explicitly grants permission through Google OAuth.

Depending on the permissions granted, School Digest may access Gmail messages, message metadata, sender information, subject lines, message content, and other information required to identify and process school-related communications.

How We Use Google User Data

Google user data is used only to provide School Digest functionality. This may include identifying school-related emails, extracting relevant dates and information, organizing school communications, and generating summaries or structured school updates for the user.

Google user data is not used for advertising, marketing, user profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the functionality of School Digest.

Data Storage

School Digest stores only information necessary to provide its functionality. Authentication credentials and OAuth tokens may be stored securely so the application can maintain an authorized connection to Google services.

School Digest does not intentionally retain Google user data longer than necessary to operate the application and provide the requested features.

Data Sharing

School Digest does not sell Google user data.

Google user data is not shared with third parties except when required to operate the application or provide functionality explicitly requested by the user.

Google API Services User Data Policy

School Digest's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and information processed by School Digest from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Revoking Access

Users may revoke School Digest's access to their Google account at any time through their Google Account security settings.
