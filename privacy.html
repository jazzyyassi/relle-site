About Relle

Relle is an app that shows a school's or community's updates (posts, dates, announcements and weekly activities) to its members. Organizers publish through the Relle admin site, or Relle organizes the newsletter they already send. This policy covers the Relle iOS app, the admin site at admin.relle.org, and the services that run them.

The short version: members don't create an account or give their name, Relle collects as little as possible, and Relle has no ads, no tracking and never sells information.

Information in the app

No account. Members join a community with an invitation code or link. The app signs in anonymously, which gives the device a random identifier. Relle doesn't ask for a name, phone number or password, and asks for an email address only to join the communities described under Email below.

Community membership. When you join, Relle records which community that anonymous identifier belongs to, so you see only that community's content. Relle counts failed code attempts for a short time to stop guessing.

Email (some communities only). Some clubs let in only their members. To join one, you enter the email address the club has for you and tap the link Relle sends to confirm it. Relle keeps the confirmed address with your anonymous identifier and that community, so the club can check it against its member list and end access for anyone who isn't on it. The club's organizers can see confirmed addresses that aren't on their list. Member lists that clubs upload are kept only in scrambled (one-way) form. Your address is deleted when you leave the community or the club ends your access.

Notifications (optional). If you turn on notifications, Relle stores your device's push notification token, your community, and the number of unread posts the app last reported (used for the badge on the app icon). Tokens are deleted when they stop working, for example when the app is removed.

Kept on your device only. Your unread markers and the calendar categories you show stay on your phone.

Usage information. To show each community's organizers how Relle is used, the app sends usage information: for example that the app was opened, which posts and pages were viewed and for how long, links tapped, dates added to a calendar, notifications opened, what was typed in search, and the app version. It carries no name, email address or device identifier. Relle stores it as daily totals for each community, together with a scrambled (one-way) code made from your anonymous identifier, so it can count how many different members were active and leave out its own test phones. Searches are kept in short form; a search that looks like an email address or a long number isn't kept, and in a search typed as a question, words that look like names are replaced. Organizers see only totals and lists of popular searches, never who did what. This information isn't sold, shared with advertisers or used to track you across other apps or websites.

Calendar. When you tap Add to Calendar, the app asks for calendar access and opens the system editor so you can add the event. Relle doesn't read or upload your calendar.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, Relle receives a technical report so the problem can be fixed: where in the code it failed, your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version, and a random installation ID created by Firebase. It contains no name, email address, phone number or community content, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Access requests. Earlier versions of the app let people ask to join with an email address and a short note. These are used only to reply to the request.

Community content

Posts, announcements, calendars and activity schedules are published by the community's organizers and may include names such as teachers or coaches. Relle shows this content only to members of that community.

Some communities have Relle organize their newsletter emails or import a schedule they publish online (for example a public Google Doc). This content is processed only to create the community's posts and calendar.

Organizers and the admin site

Organizers sign in to admin.relle.org with Google. If the admin site hits an error, a technical error report (the error message, the page and the browser type) is sent to Relle; it contains no personal information. Relle uses the account's email address to check which communities they manage, and records who published or changed content.

Google user data

To organize a community's newsletter, Relle connects, with the account owner's permission through Google OAuth, to a dedicated Gmail inbox that receives the newsletter. Relle reads only the newsletter emails (their sender, subject, date and content) to extract posts, dates, deadlines and links for that community.

Google user data is used only for this purpose. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to build profiles, and not shared except with the service providers below that process it on Relle's behalf. Authorization tokens are stored securely and access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account's security settings.

Relle's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

Relle uses a small number of providers to run the service:

  • Google Cloud and Firebase: hosting, database, anonymous sign-in, email confirmation links, the admin site, push notifications, usage information and crash reports.
  • Apple: delivers push notifications to iPhones.
  • OpenAI: reads newsletter emails to pull out posts and dates, for communities that use this. OpenAI does not use this data to train its models.

Relle doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking services from other companies. The usage information and crash reports described above are the only information Relle receives about how the app is used.

Keeping and deleting information

Community content is kept while the community uses Relle. Notification tokens are removed when they stop working. A confirmed email address is deleted when you leave that community or the club ends your access. The scrambled per-member detail behind the usage counts is deleted after two years; after that only totals are kept (for example, how many times posts were opened in a week), which can't be traced to anyone. To remove the anonymous identifier and everything on your phone, delete the app. To ask Relle to delete any information about you, email us at the address below.

Children

Relle is meant for parents, guardians and community members. It isn't directed at children, and Relle doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Members can read only their own community's content, and this is checked on Relle's servers, not just in the app. Information is encrypted in transit, and only the people who run Relle can access its systems.
