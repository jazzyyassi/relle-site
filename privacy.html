Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

About Relle

Relle is an app that shows a school's or community's updates (posts, dates, announcements and weekly activities) to its members. Organizers publish through the Relle admin site, or Relle organizes the newsletter they already send. This policy covers the Relle iOS app, the admin site at admin.relle.org, and the services that run them.

The short version: members don't create an account, Relle collects as little as possible, and Relle has no ads, no tracking and never sells information.

Information in the app

No account. Members join a community with an invitation code or link. The app signs in anonymously, which gives the device a random identifier. Relle doesn't ask for a name, email address, phone number or password.

Community membership. When you join, Relle records which community that anonymous identifier belongs to, so you see only that community's content. Relle counts failed code attempts for a short time to stop guessing.

Notifications (optional). If you turn on notifications, Relle stores your device's push notification token, your community, and the number of unread posts the app last reported (used for the badge on the app icon). Tokens are deleted when they stop working, for example when the app is removed.

Kept on your device only. Which posts you've read or saved, and which calendar categories you show, stay on your phone.

Calendar. When you tap Add to Calendar, the app asks for calendar access and opens the system editor so you can add the event. Relle doesn't read or upload your calendar.

Access requests. Earlier versions of the app let people ask to join with an email address and a short note. These are used only to reply to the request.

Community content

Posts, announcements, calendars and activity schedules are published by the community's organizers and may include names such as teachers or coaches. Relle shows this content only to members of that community.

Some communities have Relle organize their newsletter emails or import a schedule they publish online (for example a public Google Doc). This content is processed only to create the community's posts and calendar.

Organizers and the admin site

Organizers sign in to admin.relle.org with Google. Relle uses the account's email address to check which communities they manage, and records who published or changed content.

Google user data

To organize a community's newsletter, Relle connects, with the account owner's permission through Google OAuth, to a dedicated Gmail inbox that receives the newsletter. Relle reads only the newsletter emails (their sender, subject, date and content) to extract posts, dates, deadlines and links for that community.

Google user data is used only for this purpose. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to build profiles, and not shared except with the service providers below that process it on Relle's behalf. Authorization tokens are stored securely and access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account's security settings.

Relle's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

Relle uses a small number of providers to run the service:

  • Google Cloud and Firebase: hosting, database, anonymous sign-in, the admin site and push notifications.
  • Apple: delivers push notifications to iPhones.
  • OpenAI: reads newsletter emails to pull out posts and dates, for communities that use this. OpenAI does not use this data to train its models.

Relle doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking services.

Keeping and deleting information

Community content is kept while the community uses Relle. Notification tokens are removed when they stop working. To remove the anonymous identifier and everything on your phone, delete the app. To ask Relle to delete any information about you, email us at the address below.

Children

Relle is meant for parents, guardians and community members. It isn't directed at children, and Relle doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Members can read only their own community's content, and this is checked on Relle's servers, not just in the app. Information is encrypted in transit, and only the people who run Relle can access its systems.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
