Common questions

How do I access my community?

If your community requires an access code, enter the code shared by your community administrator or organizer. If you don't have one, contact the person who invited you.

Why am I missing an update?

Relle displays information made available to your community. If an update is missing or appears incorrect, contact support with the community name and a brief description.

What if the app isn't working?

Make sure you're using the latest available version, then close and reopen Relle. If the problem continues, email support and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of the issue.

How can I ask about privacy or my data?

Read our Privacy Policy, or email support with your question or request.
